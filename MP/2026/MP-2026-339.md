> Minister Spraw Zagranicznych Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej i Minister Spraw Zagranicznych Republiki Turcji, zwani dalej „Umawiającymi się Stronami”,

> \- biorąc pod uwagę wolę obu Umawiających się Stron dotyczącą ożywienia i wzmocnienia współpracy w obszarze nauki,

> \- uznając osiągnięcia Kolegium Europy w Natolinie w zakresie kształcenia kadr zaangażowanych w proces integracji europejskiej oraz dotychczasową ścisłą współpracę Umawiających się Stron z Kolegium Europy w Natolinie,

> Artykuł 1

> Strona polska sfinansuje w każdym roku akademickim do dwóch stypendiów dla obywateli Republiki Turcji, umożliwiających odbycie rocznych podyplomowych studiów w Kolegium Europy w Natolinie.

> Stypendia będą finansowane ze środków budżetu Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej, z części budżetu, której dysponentem jest Minister Spraw Zagranicznych.

> Artykuł 2

> Memorandum of Understanding between the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Poland and the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Tiirkiye on educating citizens of the Republic of Tiirkiye at the College of Europe in Natolin

> The Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Poland and the Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Turkiye, hereinafter referred to as “the Contracting Parties”,

> \- in accordance with the underlying principles of friendship, enhanced cooperation, and strategic partnership between the two States,

> \- bearing in mind consistent and regular support for the aspirations of the Republic of Tirkiye to join the European Union, especially in the light of the public administration personnel's professionalisation and enhancing knowledge about the functioning of the European Union, as well as reinforcing the expert base,

> \- being aware of the shared challenges and opportunities behind cooperation in European matters,

> \- having regard to both Contracting Parties’ readiness to reinvigorate and strengthen their cooperation in the field of science,

> \- recognising a good record of the College of Europe in Natolin on educating professionals involved in European Union integration process and the strong cooperation between the Contracting Parties and the College of Europe in Natolin,

> have agreed as follows:

> Article 1

> Each academic year, the Polish Side shall fund up to two scholarships for citizens of the Republic of Tiirkiye to take one-year postgraduate studies at the College of Europe in Natolin.

> The scholarships shall be funded from the budget of the Republic of Poland under the budget heading at the disposal of the Minister of Foreign Affairs.

> Article 2

> The following institutions of the Contracting Parties are authorised to implement the Memorandum of Understanding: - in the Republic of Poland: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs - in the Republic of Turkiye: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Directorate for European Union Affairs.

> The following are the Contracting Parties’ contact institutions for matters regarding the implementation of the Memorandum of Understanding: - in the Republic of Poland: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, - in the Republic of Tiirkiye: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Directorate for European Union Affairs.

> Article 3

> The scholarships shall be awarded to citizens of the Republic of Turkiye by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Poland following an enrolment process.

> An Enrolment Committee shall be established for the enrolment of candidates in the Republic of Turkiye, composed of representatives of the College of Europe in Natolin, the College of Europe in Bruges, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of

> the Republic of Poland, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Directorate for European Union Affairs of the Republic of Tiirkiye, and specialised universities in the Republic of Tiirkiye (with the consent of Directorate for European Union Affairs and the Universities concerned).

> Article 4

> Within the awarded scholarship, the Polish Side shall provide funds to pay for the costs of: - education at the College of Europe in Natolin, - lodging in a hall of residence, - meals in a college canteen.

> Article 5

> The funds for the scholarships granted shall be transferred by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Poland to the College of Europe in Natolin.

> Article 6

> The detailed rules and procedure of transferring the funds to pay for the costs listed in Article 4 and the rules for the citizens of the Republic of Turkiye to take up and pursue education at the College of Europe in Natolin which have not been laid down herein shall be regulated in an agreement between the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Poland and the College of Europe in Natolin.

> Article 7

> The Contracting Parties’ contact institutions shall exchange information on the dissemination in the Republic of Turkiye of information regarding the scholarships, enrolment at the College of Europe in Natolin, and possible problems related to Turkish students’ education at the College of Europe in Natolin.
