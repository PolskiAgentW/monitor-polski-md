> Protocol between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of Ukraine on the amendment to the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of Ukraine on extending a tied aid credit, done at Warsaw on September 9, 2015

> The Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of Ukraine, henceforth called the "Contracting Parties", with a view to continue the constructive economic co-operation between the Republic of Poland and Ukraine, in accordance with Article 14 of the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of Ukraine on extending a tied aid credit, done at Warsaw on September 9, 2015, have agreed as follows:

> Article 1

> 1. Article 3 paragraph 2 shall be amended to read: ,,2. The Contracts referred to in

> Article 1 of this Agreement shall be concluded by entrepreneurs having their seats in the Republic of Poland (hereinafter referred to as the "Polish entrepreneurs") and entities - residents of Ukraine, not later than on 31 December 2019".

> 2. Article 3 paragraph 3 shall be amended to read: "3, The minimum value of a single Contract subject to this Agreement is 500,000 (five hundred thousand) EUR, except the contracts for consultant's services".

> Article 2

> This Protocol is an integral part of the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of Ukraine on extending a tied aid credit, done in Warsaw on September 9, 2015, and shall be subject to approval in accordance with internal laws of each of the Contracting Parties. This shall be confirmed by exchange of notes of the Contracting Parties wherein Protocol shall come into force on the date of the receipt of the later note. This Protocol remains valid as long as the Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Poland and the Government of Ukraine on extending a tied aid credit, done at Warsaw on September 9, 2015, remains valid.

> Ukrainian and English languages, all texts being equally authentic. In case of any divergences of interpretation of the Protocol, the English text shall prevail.
